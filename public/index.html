How Emergency Fund Aquarium works

The whole app in two minutes.

The big picture

You set a savings goal. Every deposit you log fills the tank a little more, and as the water rises, sea creatures show up and stay.

Saving is the least rewarding useful thing you can do: the money leaves and nothing happens. The tank is the progress bar, and it's a much better progress bar.

Step one: set your goal

A name and a target amount. If you don't have a number, start with $1,000. It covers most of what actually goes wrong: a car repair, a vet visit, a deductible. You can change it later without losing anything.

Nothing here touches your bank. You log deposits yourself.

Step two: log deposits

Amount, date, and an optional note about where it came from. Log the small ones too. $12 counts, and the tank doesn't care where the water came from.

How the tank comes alive

Creatures arrive at percentages of your goal, not dollar amounts, so a $1,000 goal and a $20,000 goal hit the same milestones at the same points.

10%Your first fish arrives.
25%Coral starts growing.
50%A turtle. Half full.
100%Treasure found. Fully stocked.

Once a creature arrives, it stays.

What each tab is for

  • Aquarium is the tank, and where you'll spend your time.
  • History is every deposit you've logged.
  • Progress is how far along you are.
  • Fish-Dex is every creature you've met and what's still out there.
  • Add Deposit is the form.

Free and Pro

Free covers one active goal, unlimited deposits, the whole tank and the Fish-Dex, with the Ocean and Tropical biomes. Pro adds unlimited goals and four more biomes (Deep Sea, Lake, Coral Reef, Arctic), each with its own creatures. Pro is on the hosted version at emergencyfundaquarium.com.
